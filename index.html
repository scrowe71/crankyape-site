About the pilot

Logbook

These tools exist because EVE kept handing me spreadsheet problems: working out what's worth importing to a null-sec market hub, keeping track of where my stuff ended up across characters and corp hangars, and noticing in-game mail without having the client open.

Everything here runs on Cloudflare with the source on GitHub, and it reads from CCP's ESI API through EVE SSO. Your tokens stay in your own browser unless a tool says otherwise.

If something breaks or you want a feature, send me an EVE mail or open an issue on GitHub.
